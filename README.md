An Assetto Corsa mouse steer Lua script that improves driving with mouse and keyboard. This script is to solve a non-existent problem.

Installation:
1. Unzip the "extension" folder to your Assetto Corsa root folder.
2. (CSP v0.3.0 or above) Select "Mouse Steering Assist" at Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > Keyboard & Mouse
3. (CSP v0.2.12 or below) Select "Mouse Steering Assist" at Settings > Custom Shaders Patch > General Patch Settings
4. (Only do this if your CSP version is below 0.3.0) Unbind throttle and brakes keys at Settings > Assetto Corsa > Controls

How to change settings:
1. Head over to "assettocorsa\extension\lua\mouse-steering\MSA"
2. Open "assist.lua" with any text editor
3. Change values however you want according to your preference!

How to Use:
In game, turn your mouse horizontally to rotate the steering wheel. Press the keys you've assigned to press pedals. Press shift to forced throttle or brake and scroll mouse wheel to change pedal percentage.

Known issues/bugs & possible future update:
- The script doesn't work as intended with some RSS cars. (Need help.)
- compatibility issue with CSP v0.2.12 or below version.
- Smoothness can't be 0%
- All mouse steering script is bugged in CSP v0.3.0-preview361. (It is a bug of CSP, not my script. As default mouse steering scripts have the same issue.)
Force feedback is turned off by default.
